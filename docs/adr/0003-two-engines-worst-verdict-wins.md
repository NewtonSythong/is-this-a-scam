# Two engines split by what each is good at, and the more alarming Verdict wins

An earlier plan had deterministic rules produce the Verdict and the language model merely phrase it. That is close to inverted on the cases that matter. "Hi Mum, this is my new number, my old phone broke" contains no link, no urgency keyword and no payment request, and scores zero on rules — while a genuine bank message about unusual card activity trips urgency, authority and call-to-action at once. So the Artifact Check owns what can be looked up and verified, the Narrative Check owns the story being told, and the Verdict is the more alarming of the two rather than an average — averaging would let a clean-looking link talk down a textbook impersonation. The binding constraint is that a Reason may only cite a Signal one of the two engines actually produced, which keeps the model from inventing evidence and makes the Artifact Check's contribution deterministically testable. When the Narrative Check fails or times out, the Check still returns a Verdict from the Artifact Check alone; the app never ends in "sorry, try again", because a Checker reads that as "it's probably fine".
