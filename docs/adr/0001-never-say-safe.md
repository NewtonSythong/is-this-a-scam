# Three Verdicts, none of them "safe", and no number shown to the Checker

The two errors are not symmetric: a false reassurance costs a Checker their savings, while a false warning costs them a phone call. So the app has no green tick and no fourth Verdict meaning "this is fine" — the best available outcome is "we can't tell", which is honest about what an automated Check can actually establish. A Suspicion Score is still computed internally, because thresholds have to be tuned against something and tests have to assert on something, but it is never rendered for a Checker and is exposed only in a developer view. A percentage was rejected specifically because it is negotiable: "70% likely a scam" is read by a frightened person as a 30% chance their grandchild really is stranded, and they act on the 30%. An AI disclaimer stating that the Check is automated and can be wrong is shown regardless of Verdict.
